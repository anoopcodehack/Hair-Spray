#  Hairspray — Salon & Spa

> **Style. Care. Relax. Repeat.**

Hairspray is a modern **Salon & Spa** platform designed to bring professional hair care, beauty treatments, skincare, and relaxation services together in one place.

From a precision haircut and styling session to a relaxing massage or rejuvenating face spa, Hairspray is built around providing customers with a complete personal-care experience.

---

##  About Hairspray

**Hairspray** is more than just a hair salon.

It combines professional **hair styling, grooming, skincare, spa treatments, and relaxation services** into a single experience.

The platform is designed to help customers:

-  Explore haircuts and styling services
-  Discover professional hair-care treatments
-  Book face spa and skincare treatments
-  Explore massage and relaxation services
-  Discover additional beauty and grooming services
-  Schedule appointments easily
-  View available services and pricing
-  Access the salon experience through a modern, responsive interface
